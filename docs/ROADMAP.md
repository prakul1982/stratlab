# Roadmap

What's planned next. The aim is one place an investor can trust for a company, so precision comes before breadth:
every number from a filing, every claim tied to a quote, and a clear note when something couldn't be read.

## Planned

- **Options backtesting:** option chains are being recorded; backtesting opens once enough history has built up.
- **Currency options on the cross pairs** (EURUSD, GBPUSD, USDJPY).
- **Local-currency payments** in more countries.
- **Every feature checked on real data at scale:** AI reads across the Nifty 500 and every industry, and figures
  compared against their sources.
- **Zerodha for every user**, once Zerodha approves a multi-user app.

## Known gaps

Each shows a note on the page instead of a wrong number.

- Calls with no transcript (audio or video only), and investor sites that show nothing without JavaScript.
- Industry measures (occupancy, NIM, ARPU and the like) appear only when the company states them.
- US companies: no call transcripts are filed, so the report card reads earnings releases only.
- Briefs are written once, after each market's close.
