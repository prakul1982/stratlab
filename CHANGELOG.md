# Changelog

What shipped, newest first, grouped by month. One or two lines per release.

## November 2026

### 5 November 2026: launch polish (Releases 5 to 10)

- Calm loading bars on every card that waits, and redesigned PowerPoint and PDF decks for company deep dives and themes.
- Feedback from the account menu, live Market Mood while the market is open, a weekly review on the blog for India and the US, and charts that wait out a busy price source instead of failing.

### 4 November 2026: Releases 1 to 4

- Better AI research answers, and Themes built from official sector lists that answer at once and then fill in.
- Steadier sign-in, money imports that keep their dates, and jobs that restart by themselves.

## October 2026

### 29 October 2026: Themes as a deep dive

- A theme is now a set of tables worked out from company figures: each layer of the value chain with its revenue, margins and returns.

### 28 October 2026: Market Watch, public pages and live numbers

- Market Watch: a panel of lists and charts that stays open from page to page, with a chart workspace.
- Public company pages with share-price charts, a redrawn blog, and every page with live market numbers refreshing each minute while its market is open.

### 26 October 2026: the strategy library for every market

- The strategy library covers every market, Mutual funds reads as one list, and many pages were tidied after a visual review.

### 25 October 2026: IPOs

- IPOs for India and the US: open, upcoming and listed issues, the bids while an issue is open, and summaries of the offer documents.

### 8 October 2026: View as a plan

- The site owner can see the app as a Free, Basic or Pro user sees it.

### 6 and 7 October 2026: the UI overhaul and My space

- Every page rebuilt on one design kit, a new left menu with ⌘K search, and My space as a home with what needs you today.
- Connect a broker once and use it everywhere; clearer emails, market time zones, accessibility and pricing wording.

### 5 October 2026: options, funds and charts

- Price charts, options Greeks and what-if, strikes picked by delta or premium, India VIX, stock futures and lending fees.
- Fund behaviour and costs, ETF price against NAV, stock and ETF SIP tests, fixed-income rates and business updates in numbers.

### 4 and 5 October 2026: three spaces

- Trade, Invest and Money spaces, each with its own home; positioning, market breadth and a trade journal.
- StratLab in your AI assistant (Pro).

### Earlier in October

- **Tax report**, exchange surveillance lists, corporate actions and dividends, and deals and insider trades for India.
- **Stock screens**, stock alerts, My Holdings, a results calendar, and plain numbers in place of scores.
- **Public company pages**, shareable company cards, and invite links that give both sides a free month of Basic.
- **Newsletters**, research reads that stay factual, and BSE-only companies.
- **US companies** get the full deep dive; Indian currency derivatives; amounts shown in the unit people use.
- **Payments from anywhere**, with GST invoices, and new prices and plan limits.
- **Built around what you came for:** one question at the start (investing, trading or both) sets the menu and home page.
- Many reliability and speed improvements, and browser tests across the whole app.

## September 2026

- **Company deep dive** on every Indian company page (Pro), from the figures and the company's own documents.
- **Sector rotation** (Pro): every sector against the market as leading, weakening, lagging or improving.
- **Filings and red flags** for your watchlist companies and on every Indian company page.
- **Stage 2 + Supertrend:** a market-stage indicator for rules, and a scan of your watchlist.

- **Research, and every market live**, a real landing page, and a new logo.
